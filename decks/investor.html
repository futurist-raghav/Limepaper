The Problem

Two problems that solve each other

Packaging buyers need an alternative

  • India's single-use-plastic restrictions and FMCG ESG mandates are pushing procurement toward substitutes
  • Wood-pulp paper carries ~200 tonnes of water and ~2,900 kWh of energy per tonne — a real cost and ESG liability
  • Wood prices have risen ~88% recently; India pays a premium vs. international buyers

Rajasthan has a waste crisis, not a resource

  • Kishangarh's marble-slurry dumping yard takes a truck roughly every 10 minutes at peak
  • CPCB and NGT have flagged groundwater and soil contamination from this slurry
  • Processors actively want offtake — feedstock is a liability looking for a buyer, not a scarce input

Risks — Addressed Directly, Not Glossed Over

What we're watching, and what we're doing about it

Not "plastic-free"

~18% of the product is HDPE, which only photodegrades — not biodegrades. "Tree-free, water-free" is the defensible claim; "plastic-free" is not, and we say so upfront rather than let a buyer discover it.

GST/HSN classification is open

Could be taxed as "plastic sheet" rather than "paper" — a material swing in margin. An advance ruling is a pre-condition before capital moves, not an afterthought.

The one India precedent went dormant

Ston2pack, the only branded India stone-paper player, has been quiet since 2022–23 under arguably better conditions than today. Diligence on why is our first task, before any other capital is spent.

Capital precedes revenue

Stage 1 capex is drawn before the plant sells anything. We've built hard gates — signed LOIs, vendor quotes, a debt-service model at 30/50/70% utilisation — before equipment is ordered.

Where We Stand On Fundraising

We looked at pre-seed equity, and said not yet

We ran a structured, adversarial review of an Antler India pre-seed application (~₹2–4cr for 9–11% equity) against our debt-financed Stage 1 plan. The finding: applying now would price the company at a valuation it hasn't earned yet, and convert a recoverable asset risk into permanent equity dilution and governance obligations — before our own governance structure is even finalized.

What we're doing instead

Confirming whether CGTMSE's own collateral-substitution mechanics can reduce our shop-pledge exposure — the actual problem outside equity would need to solve.

When this changes

A manufacturing-focused investor at a valuation grounded in comparable deals, or Stage 2's own gates being met on schedule, both reopen this conversation on our terms, not a program's.
