The Material

What it's made of, and what that gets you

Composition

  • ≈80% ground limestone/marble waste (CaCO₃)
  • ≈18% HDPE as binder
  • ≈2% additives
  • Available as sheet, roll, or converted into your product spec

Practical properties

  • Tear-resistant, water-resistant — holds up where wood-pulp paper softens or tears
  • Smooth, dense surface — takes print cleanly
  • No wood pulp, no bleaching agents, no process water
  • Comparable weight/thickness ranges to standard paper stock

Why Switch

The case, in three parts

Regulatory fit

India's single-use-plastic restrictions are tightening. Stone paper is a defensible substitute for plastic-based packaging in categories where compliance officers are actively looking for alternatives.

ESG story, honestly told

Tree-free and water-free are true, verifiable claims. We do not claim "plastic-free" or "compostable" — the product is ~18% HDPE and photodegrades, it does not biodegrade. We'd rather you hear that from us than discover it later.

Input cost stability

Wood pulp prices have risen sharply and India often pays a premium versus international buyers. Our input is a waste stream with a stable, low local cost.

Certifications & Compliance

Where we stand — no rounding up

What's confirmed

  • GST/Udyam registration in progress at incorporation
  • PCB Consent to Establish filed as part of Stage 1 setup
  • Batch-level traceability built into QC from day one

What's still open

GST/HSN classification for stone paper is not yet settled — it could be taxed as "plastic sheet" rather than "paper," which affects landed cost. We're seeking an advance ruling before scaling output, and we'll tell you the answer as soon as we have it, not after you've committed volume.

Supply Reliability

Why feedstock isn't the risk it looks like

Feedstock is abundant, not scarce

  • Kishangarh's marble-slurry yard receives a truck roughly every 10 minutes at peak
  • Processors want offtake — it's a disposal cost for them, not a resource they're protecting
  • Multiple processor relationships, not single-source dependency

Site logistics

  • Plant sited near the marble belt — feedstock travels a short distance, not cross-state
  • HDPE is the one nationally-sourced input — a standard petrochemical commodity available from multiple suppliers
