**Elevator pitch (≤500 chars):**
> Limepaper makes tree-free, water-free "stone paper" — a packaging and stationery substrate made from ~80% ground limestone/marble waste instead of wood pulp — manufactured in Rajasthan's marble belt. We turn a real pollution liability (marble slurry CPCB/NGT already flag as hazardous) into cheap, secure feedstock, and sell it as raw substrate to converters/brands, plus a small range of finished notebooks, cartons and bags — riding India's plastic bans and FMCG ESG mandates.

**What problem are you solving:**
> Rajasthan's marble industry produces large volumes of limestone/marble slurry that CPCB and NGT classify as a pollution hazard, with no cheap disposal route — a liability looking for an offtaker. Meanwhile India's single-use-plastic rules and FMCG ESG mandates are pushing packaging/stationery buyers toward alternatives to both plastic and wood-pulp paper, but no Indian player currently owns a captive, mine-mouth stone-paper supply chain (the one branded attempt, Ston2pack, has gone quiet since 2022–23). We convert that waste stream into a tree-free, water-free substrate that's also cheaper than wood-pulp paper at current wood prices.

**Current traction:**
> Pre-revenue, pre-plant as of Sep 2026 — Stage 1 (₹1.5cr entry-tier extrusion + converting plant) is at the financing/vendor-quoting stage, not yet commissioned. Metrics we're tracking ahead of commissioning: signed LOIs from B2B anchor buyers (targeting 3–5 before the equipment order), firm vendor quotes for the extrusion line, feedstock and HDPE (3 each), and GST/HSN advance-ruling status. No revenue, users, or paid customers yet.

> - IPV reserves the right to screen submitted entries and/or disqualify any entry for any reason, in its sole and absolute discretion.
> - By submitting an entry, each startup grants permission for the entry to be posted on IPV's and sponsors' website(s) and any social media platform (including but not limited to Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Twitter, YouTube) and/or sponsor-generated email, at any time, in perpetuity, worldwide, for viewing.
> - The decisions of the judges are final and binding on all matters relating to selection of winners.
> - Disbursement of INR 1 Cr – INR 1.5 Cr to winners is contingent on successful completion of due diligence. Only if due diligence yields a positive result are the proposals presented to potential investors.
> - IPV is poised to invest INR 1 Cr – INR 1.5 Cr, securing a 12.5%–17.6% equity stake respectively in the winners, contingent on successful completion of due diligence.

### What these terms actually mean for Limepaper — read before submitting

- **This is a real, priced equity offer, not just a mentorship program.** ₹1 Cr for 17.6% implies a post-money valuation of **≈₹5.68 Cr**; ₹1.5 Cr for 12.5% implies **≈₹12 Cr** post-money. Both are far below the ₹150 Cr post-money the plan's own Stage 2 ask assumes (`01_Core_Idea_Pitch_Summary.md`) — not comparable instruments (idea-stage program vs. a Series-A-shaped raise), but worth having the number in your head before a term sheet conversation.
- **It's the same order of magnitude as the bootstrapped Stage 1 ₹1.5cr ceiling** (`07_Capital_Plan.md` §9.1) — if IPV actually wins and funds this, it's a real alternative to collateral-backed debt against the family shop, not just a "nice to have" accelerator badge. Worth deciding, if you get this far, whether IPV equity at ~12.5–17.6% beats risking the ₹70L shop on a term loan — a genuinely different trade-off than what the plan currently assumes ("no external equity" in Stage 1).
- **Everything perpetuity/social-media clause:** submitting hands IPV and sponsors a worldwide, perpetual right to publish your entry — deck, pitch, whatever's submitted — across their channels. Standard for competition entries, but it means anything sensitive shouldn't go in past what's already public-facing; the unit-economics and capital-structure detail in this deck is more candid than most pitch decks would be, by design (matches the plan's own "advisor not cheerleader" standard) — a call worth making consciously, not by default.
- **Disbursement and equity are both contingent on due diligence**, and due diligence only *then* leads to being presented to investors — winning the program is a gate to a process, not a funding event itself. Don't read "winner" as "funded."
- **Judging decisions are final** — no appeal path mentioned. Nothing to do about that beyond noting it.
