## The one-line version

Tree-free, water-free "stone paper" (ground limestone/marble waste + HDPE) made near Rajasthan's marble belt, where the raw material is currently a pollution liability, not a scarce input. Team of three: two 18-year-old founders plus a senior family member (father) handling operations and vendor relationships, bringing ~4 years of carton-industry experience.

**Stage 1** is a bootstrapped **₹1.5 crore** extrusion + converting plant — no external equity, funded by a real ₹70 lakh family-owned shop (clear title) plus ≈₹10 lakh in silver and collateral-backed term debt. ₹1.5 crore is a hard borrowing ceiling; no new debt until ≥50% of it is repaid from operations. Extrudes its own sheet, refines its own feedstock in-house, and converts sheet into finished product (notebooks, cartons, conditionally paper bags) on one site from day one, with ring-fenced salary/emergency reserves built into the ceiling (Doc 7 §9.2.1).

**Stage 2** is a **₹15 crore raise for 10% equity**, sized against the ₹17.2 crore published benchmark for a 5 TPD turnkey plant (Doc 7 §9.3).

Machine list rebuilt around what's essential now: in-house feedstock refining, entry-tier extrusion, core converting (notebooks/cartons), and a paper bag machine conditional on a glue/rope-adhesion trial (Doc 17 §17.2.2). Label die-cutter deferred; bakery/QSR wraps tested on existing equipment pending FSSAI; wallpaper deferred. Full risk read: Doc 14 §6.
