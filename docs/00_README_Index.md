## The one-line version

Tree-free, water-free "stone paper" (ground limestone/marble waste + HDPE) made near Rajasthan's marble belt, where the raw material is currently a pollution liability, not a scarce input. Team of three: two 18-year-old founders plus a senior family member (father) handling operations and vendor relationships. **Stage 1 is a bootstrapped ₹1.5 crore extrusion + converting plant** — no external equity, funded from family capital, ≈₹10 lakh in silver and collateral-backed term debt, with ₹1.5 crore a hard ceiling on borrowing and no new debt until at least 50% of it is repaid from operations. It extrudes its own sheet and converts it into finished product on one site from day one. **Stage 2 is a ₹15 crore raise for 10% equity**, sized against the ₹17.2 crore published benchmark for a 5 TPD turnkey plant (Doc 7, §9.3).

*Revised 23 August 2026: the earlier ₹10–15 lakh converting-only first stage was removed from the plan. Docs 7, 8 and 14 carry the full consequences — most importantly that capex now lands before revenue, which [14_Founder_Reality_Check.md](14_Founder_Reality_Check.md) §6 treats as the plan's biggest open risk.*
