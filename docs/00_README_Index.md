## The one-line version

Tree-free, water-free "stone paper" (ground limestone/marble waste + HDPE) made near Rajasthan's marble belt, where the raw material is currently a pollution liability, not a scarce input. Team of three: two 18-year-old founders plus a senior family member (father) handling operations and vendor relationships. Current capital is ≈₹10 lakh in silver, and total borrowed capital across the next 36 months is capped at **₹1.5 crore — a hard ceiling, not a target.** The plan is a **minimum-setup, heavily automated converting factory** (₹10–15 lakh — Doc 7, Stage 1) that starts real production and sales immediately, self-funds from its own profit for 12–24 months, then raises the rest of that ₹1.5 crore to backward-integrate into making the raw stone paper itself (Stage 2) — with no further capital raised after that until at least 50% of the Stage 2 loan is repaid from operations.
