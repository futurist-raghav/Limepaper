# B2B Client Acquisition Playbook

*Part of the Limepaper business plan — see [00_README_Index.md](00_README_Index.md) for the full document set.*

## 11.1 Who to target, in priority order

1. **Packaging converters/printers who already serve FMCG/pharma brands** — sell them stone paper as a substrate input; they do the conversion to final packaging. Lower customer-acquisition cost than pitching FMCG brands directly.

2. **E-commerce mailer/label buyers** — high volume, standardised specs, good for proving consistent quality at scale.

3. **Corporate gifting and premium print houses** — smaller volume, higher margin, good early revenue while you scale.

4. **Industrial labels & tags converters** — the fastest-growing sub-segment per [02_Market_Opportunity.md](02_Market_Opportunity.md) §3.2, high-margin, low-volume entry point.

5. **Institutional/government** — school and college notebook tenders, PSU corporate-gifting programmes — price-sensitive but large, reliable volume once you're a qualified vendor.

## 11.2 Tactics

* **Lead with lab data, not a pitch deck.** Procurement teams need tear-strength, water-resistance and GSM-equivalent numbers before they'll sample. Get third-party lab certification early ([08_Implementation_Roadmap.md](08_Implementation_Roadmap.md) Phase B, step 11) and put it on one page.

* **List on IndiaMART/TradeIndia.** Every competitor in [04_Competitive_Landscape.md](04_Competitive_Landscape.md) has a presence there — low-cost inbound lead generation for India B2B, and where converters actually search for new suppliers.

* **Exhibit at or attend India's packaging/print trade shows** (e.g. PackPlus, PrintPack India) — where converters and FMCG packaging buyers actually source new substrates. Budget for at least one show once you have sellable samples.

* **Pre-sell before you scale.** Map 3–5 target anchor B2B customers and get LOIs before committing Phase 1 capex ([07_Capital_Plan.md](07_Capital_Plan.md) §9.3) — both a sales and a fundraising tactic, since lenders want to see offtake.

* **Use the sustainability/ESG hook directly with brand procurement teams**, not just converters — many FMCG companies now run packaging-sustainability scorecards that a "tree-free, water-free, made from diverted marble waste" story can score well against. Ask early B2B contacts whether they have an ESG/packaging-sustainability lead you can talk to.

* **Turn the Ston2pack gap into your pitch.** If Ston2pack has genuinely gone quiet ([04_Competitive_Landscape.md](04_Competitive_Landscape.md) §5.4), any converter or brand that sampled their product in 2021–23 is a warm lead with existing category awareness — worth trying to identify.

## 11.3 Sequencing against the rest of the plan

This playbook runs continuously starting Week 6 of [08_Implementation_Roadmap.md](08_Implementation_Roadmap.md) (Phase E) — it isn't a later-stage activity. Your first 2–3 signed LOIs double as:

1. Revenue validation for Phase 0.
2. Fundraising collateral for the Phase 1 raise ([07_Capital_Plan.md](07_Capital_Plan.md) §9.3) — lenders and investors want to see offtake before they'll write a cheque.
