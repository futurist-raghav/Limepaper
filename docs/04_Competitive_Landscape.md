## 5.2 India players

| Company | Model | Notes |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Stone Paper Printing & Packaging India LLP ("Ston2pack") | B2B + B2C (as of 2021–22) | Positioned itself as "India's first and only" stone paper printing & packaging company. B2B: FMCG/pharma packaging, corrugated stone boxes/boards, industrial goods transport. B2C: "Whitestone Stationeries" sub-brand — diaries, notebooks, planners. **Status update (Aug 2026): likely dormant** — see §5.4. | Karst (imported) | B2C via e-commerce | Premium global notebook brand available in India through marketplaces — useful as a B2C price/positioning benchmark even though not India-manufactured. |

*No India competitor advertises a captive, mine-mouth position inside the Rajasthan marble belt — a genuine differentiation angle: "we solve a local pollution problem and turn it into India's most credibly-sourced stone paper" is a story none of the above can tell as convincingly.*

## 5.3 B2B vs B2C — how to sequence go-to-market

* B2B first is the more common playbook (packaging leads at ~38–50% share) — target FMCG/pharma converters, e-commerce mailer/label buyers, corporate-gifting/print houses. Absorbs volume fastest, validates unit economics with fewer SKUs.
* B2C stationery (notebooks, diaries, planners) is lower-volume but higher-margin and brand-building — Ston2pack's Whitestone and global Karst both use it this way: a consumer brand that also markets the B2B business's sustainability credentials.
* Sensible sequence for a new entrant: launch with 1–2 B2B packaging/label SKUs to build cash flow and prove the plant, then layer in a D2C stationery line that doubles as a marketing vehicle — "made from the marble waste of Kishangarh" is a strong consumer story for corporate gifting and premium retail. Full B2C setup: [08_Implementation_Roadmap.md](08_Implementation_Roadmap.md) Phase F.

## 5.4 Reading the Ston2pack Signal — Opportunity or Warning?

Ston2pack (founded ~2021 by Mayur Umbratkar) is the only known India company with a *branded* stone-paper business spanning B2B packaging and B2C stationery (Whitestone Stationeries), with real trade press (PrintWeek India) and LinkedIn traction through 2022.

**Likely dormant:** copyright notice frozen at "© 2021," product photos carry 2020–21 timestamps, no press or LinkedIn activity 2023–2026 — consistent with active 2021–2023, no traces since. The LLP still shows registered (Thane, Maharashtra) on company-search sites, which is normal for a stalled entity never formally wound up.

Two readings worth holding as partly-both-true rather than picking one:

* **Opportunity:** the one company that proved India demand exists appears to have stalled — the §5.3 white space may be more open than it looks, and dormant relationships (converters/FMCG buyers who sampled their product, ex-employees) may be worth reaching.
* **Warning:** a real, recent India stone-paper venture stalled after 2–3 years. Worth understanding why — HDPE cost volatility, working-capital strain, quality-at-scale failure, or the founder simply moving on are all plausible, each implying a different mitigation.

**Cheap diligence worth doing early:** try to reach the founder or anyone who bought from Ston2pack; check the LLP's MCA/ROC filing status for distress/closure signs; if truly inactive, any 2021–23 buyer is a warm lead with existing category awareness ([09_B2B_Client_Acquisition.md](09_B2B_Client_Acquisition.md) §11.2).
