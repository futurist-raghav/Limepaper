# Fundraise Thesis — What Limepaper Is Actually Raising, and the Antler Decision

*Part of the Limepaper business plan — see [00_README_Index.md](00_README_Index.md). Added 11 Sep 2026, following a second 5-advisor LLM Council review (Contrarian, First Principles Thinker, Expansionist, Outsider, Executor, each peer-reviewed by the other four, chairman-synthesized) run specifically on whether Limepaper should apply to Antler India's pre-seed program. Same methodology as [14_Founder_Reality_Check.md](14_Founder_Reality_Check.md) §7 — see that section for the sequencing-risk finding this decision sits on top of.*

## 19.0 The honest one-line answer

**Limepaper is not raising pre-seed equity right now.** Stage 1 stays a bootstrapped, collateral-backed ₹1.5 crore debt plan ([07_Capital_Plan.md](07_Capital_Plan.md) §9.2). This doc exists because the founder explicitly asked "what are we raising, how much, and how is it used over 18 months" — the real answer required first resolving *whether* to raise pre-seed equity from Antler India at all. The council said no, as currently scoped. §19.4 gives the actual 18-month use-of-funds plan for the money Limepaper *is* deploying.

## 19.1 The Antler question and how it was resolved

Antler India runs a cohort/residency program (Bengaluru): founders join, validate/build over roughly 6-8 weeks, then present to an investment committee for a pre-seed check — standard deal ≈₹2 crore for ≈9% equity, some tracks up to ₹4cr/11%. It's explicitly built for pre-launch, pre-idea teams; existing teams with a built plan can sometimes apply, but this varies by cohort and hasn't been confirmed with Antler directly.

Limepaper already has the opposite profile: a complete idea, a 3-person team, and 18 documents of market sizing, unit economics, a capital plan, a factory operations plan, and a 10-SKU product catalog — far more developed than Antler's typical applicant.

The council's verdict (full transcript presented in chat 11 Sep 2026, same 5-advisor → peer-review → chairman-synthesis method as [14_Founder_Reality_Check.md](14_Founder_Reality_Check.md) §7):

* **Applicant-shape mismatch, unanimous.** All five advisors converged that Antler's diligence process isn't built to evaluate a finished manufacturing plan — the mismatch is a real cost (6-8 weeks, both founders relocated to Bengaluru, the full plan exposed to an investment committee that may reject it), not a formality to push through.
* **The valuation doesn't hold up.** ₹2-4cr for 9-11% implies a ~₹20-22cr pre-money valuation on a pre-revenue, pre-plant company — a number four of five advisors flagged as indefensible against the plan's own ₹150cr Stage 2 post-money assumption 12-18 months out ([07_Capital_Plan.md](07_Capital_Plan.md) §9.3).
* **Peer review resolved the one real disagreement.** The Expansionist argued Antler equity is a clean, non-recourse substitute for the shop-collateralized loan — "de-risk the family's only major asset for the cost of equity the company doesn't have yet anyway." All 5 independent peer reviewers (anonymized cross-review) rejected this framing: giving up 9-11% permanent equity at an unproven valuation isn't free just because it isn't debt — it converts a *recoverable* asset risk into a *permanent* control risk (board seat, reporting obligations) inside a family business whose governance is still an informal draft ([15_Equity_Split_Structure.md](15_Equity_Split_Structure.md)).
* **The blind spot every advisor missed, caught only in peer review:** nobody had checked whether CGTMSE's own collateral-substitution mechanics might already reduce or remove the need to pledge the ₹70L shop at all — which would mean the problem this whole Antler detour was trying to solve might already be solvable inside the loan structure that exists today.

**Recommendation:** don't apply to Antler as currently scoped. Not a rejection of raising capital differently in the future — a rejection of this specific program, because the process cost is being paid for a fit nobody has confirmed exists.

## 19.2 The one thing to do first — before Antler, before anything else

Ask the CGTMSE-empanelled lender directly: **is the ₹70L shop pledge actually required, or does CGTMSE's collateral-substitution guarantee reduce or remove that requirement?** CGTMSE exists specifically to reduce collateral dependency for MSME borrowers ([07_Capital_Plan.md](07_Capital_Plan.md) §9.2.2 already lists it as the mechanism covering the gap beyond hard collateral) — nobody in this plan has tested whether it can cover more of that gap than currently assumed. If it can, [14_Founder_Reality_Check.md](14_Founder_Reality_Check.md) §7's core risk (the shop pledged as a binary, all-or-nothing outcome) shrinks without needing Antler, a leasing arrangement, or any other new instrument. This is a phone call, not a project — do it before the six items in Doc 14 §"The six things to resolve" consume more diligence time.

## 19.3 If not Antler, what about the alternative instruments the council flagged?

Two prior council rounds (Doc 14 §7 and this one) have now independently flagged the same gap: **no alternative financing instrument has been priced with real numbers**, only named. Once §19.2's CGTMSE question is answered, get actual terms — not estimates — on:

* **Equipment leasing/hire-purchase on the extrusion + calendering line** (the single largest capex line, ₹55–70L per [07_Capital_Plan.md](07_Capital_Plan.md) §9.2.1) — ask the vendor directly during the quote process, per Doc 14 §"The six things to resolve" item 3.
* **Vendor financing on the core converting line.**
* **A milestone-gated phased drawdown** structured against the loan itself, rather than the full ₹1.5cr disbursed and collateralized on day one.

None of these are decisions to make now — they're quotes to get in parallel with the existing loan-sanction process, so that if the CGTMSE answer in §19.2 is unfavorable, there's a priced fallback instead of a named-but-unpriced one.

## 19.5 What "after 18 months" actually means — Stage 2 is a gate, not a date

The founder's original framing ("18 months after, how will that raise be used") assumes a raise happens on a calendar. It doesn't — Stage 2 (₹15cr for 10% equity, [07_Capital_Plan.md](07_Capital_Plan.md) §9.3) is explicitly gated on conditions, not time:

* 12+ months of real Stage 1 production and sales data — cost per tonne, yield, utilisation, repeat orders.
* 3–5 anchor B2B accounts with repeat purchase history, not LOIs.
* Stage 1 debt serviced on schedule from operations.
* A resolved GST/HSN classification.
* An answer to why Ston2pack stalled (Doc 14 §1).

If 18 months out those conditions aren't met, the honest answer is Stage 2 doesn't open yet — not that the ask shrinks or the story gets softened. [14_Founder_Reality_Check.md](14_Founder_Reality_Check.md) §6 already flags that a ₹15cr ask against an unproven plant "won't survive a first diligence conversation" — that's still true regardless of what happens with Antler.

## 19.6 When would the Antler-or-equivalent answer change?

Not never — the council's finding was about *this specific program, at this specific stage*, not equity financing as a category. Revisit if:

* §19.2's CGTMSE answer comes back unfavorable (the shop pledge is genuinely required, with no substitution) **and** the §19.3 alternative instruments don't close the gap either — at that point, a small, correctly-priced pre-seed round becomes a real option to re-evaluate, not a fallback taken by default.
* A specific investor confirmed to fund capital-intensive manufacturing (not a generalist tech pre-seed fund) approaches at a valuation grounded in comparable manufacturing-stage deals, not a program-standard check size. [23_Startup_Roadmap_Investor_Outreach.md](23_Startup_Roadmap_Investor_Outreach.md) §23.3 has a verified starting list of manufacturing/climate-hardware-focused Indian funds worth that outreach.
* Antler (or a similar program) is contacted directly per Doc 14 §7's Executor-flagged step and confirms — in writing, from a person, not inferred from a web search — that an existing team with a built plan fits a current cohort. That single confirmation changes the applicant-shape-mismatch finding this whole verdict rests on.
