# Implementation Roadmap (Three-Person Team)

*Part of the Limepaper business plan — see [00_README_Index.md](00_README_Index.md) for the full document set. Revised 23 August 2026 for the restructured capital plan: the converting-only first stage is gone; Stage 1 is the extrusion plant itself.*

Organised by phase, roughly timed, for your actual team of three. "F1/F2" = either 18-year-old co-founder — assign by strength. "P" = your father, the team's senior member — see [06_Core_Team_Experts.md](06_Core_Team_Experts.md) §8.0 for why his role is formalised, not informal. This roadmap reflects the strategy in [07_Capital_Plan.md](07_Capital_Plan.md): build a bootstrapped ₹1.5 crore extrusion + converting plant, run it to a real operating record, then raise ₹15 crore to scale to 5 TPD.

**The one structural change to keep in front of you:** capex now comes *before* revenue. There is no cheap trading stage to learn on. Everything in Phases A–C exists to de-risk a ₹1.5 crore commitment made before the business has sold anything.

## Phase A — Foundation (Weeks 1–6)

1. Finalise the core idea one-pager in [01_Core_Idea_Pitch_Summary.md](01_Core_Idea_Pitch_Summary.md) — fill in founder bios (all three of you).

2. Register the entity — **Pvt Ltd, not LLP**, since a ₹15 crore equity round is now an explicit part of the plan ([07_Capital_Plan.md](07_Capital_Plan.md) §9.3) and LLPs are a poor vehicle for it. All three of you formally on it (§8.0), with the dual-class structure in [15_Equity_Split_Structure.md](15_Equity_Split_Structure.md) settled *before* outside money is discussed.

3. Reserve and trademark-file the brand name.

4. Open a business bank account and set up basic bookkeeping (CA-led).

5. P: open the financing conversation early — silver-backed loan terms ([07_Capital_Plan.md](07_Capital_Plan.md) §9.2.2), CGTMSE-backed term loan appetite with 2–3 banks, and the District Industries Centre on RIPS 2024 / Circular Economy Scheme 2025 slabs. **Lead time on sanction is the long pole of this whole plan** — start it in week one, not after the quotes are in.

6. F1: start supplier-side research — micronized stone-paper-grade CaCO₃ suppliers in Kishangarh/Rajsamand, and HDPE distributors linked to the national petrochemical majors. F2: start customer-side research (packaging converters, notebook buyers, potential B2B leads). P: start scouting a leasable industrial unit with adequate power sanction and building local vendor/landlord relationships.

## Phase B — Core Team & Research (Weeks 4–12, overlaps Phase A)

7. **Engage the DPR consultant properly** ([06_Core_Team_Experts.md](06_Core_Team_Experts.md) role #1) — a ₹1.5 crore collateral-backed term loan needs a bankable Detailed Project Report. This is no longer deferrable.

8. **Engage the polymer/extrusion process engineer** (role #5) as a paid consultant now, not later. HDPE–CaCO₃ compounding and sheet extrusion is the core technical risk of the business you're now building on day one — temperature profile, die design, additive ratios. Get it wrong and you own a ₹1 crore machine producing brittle or discoloured sheet.

9. Engage the compliance/EHS consultant (role #3) to map the **full manufacturing** consent path — Pollution Control Board Consent to Establish *and* Consent to Operate, plus Udyam/MSME, GST and factory/trade licences. Extrusion triggers all of it; budget the timeline, not just the fee.

10. Run a competitive-intelligence pass: get samples from Karst, Chinese stone-paper suppliers, and — if reachable — Ston2pack ([04_Competitive_Landscape.md](04_Competitive_Landscape.md) §5.4) to benchmark real finished-product quality and pricing.

11. Try to reach the Ston2pack founder or anyone who bought from them — cheap diligence on why the category's one branded India player went quiet. **Do this before any equipment order**, not alongside it.

12. Get three firm quotes each for: (a) the extrusion + calendering line (site visit and a reference customer for each — [12_Vendor_Hiring_Due_Diligence.md](12_Vendor_Hiring_Due_Diligence.md)), (b) converting/finishing equipment, (c) micronized CaCO₃ powder at stone-paper grade, (d) HDPE resin, (e) lab testing for tear/waterproof certification.

## Phase C — Process, Compliance & Pre-Sales (Weeks 8–20)

13. File every registration identified in step 9, and start the Consent to Establish application — this sits on the critical path to commissioning.

14. Lock feedstock supply: a micronized CaCO₃ agreement with a local unit ([07_Capital_Plan.md](07_Capital_Plan.md) §9.2.3(a)) and an HDPE supply relationship. **Lab-test every source before committing**, including repeat batches.

15. Set up incoming QC (powder whiteness, particle size, contaminant screening; resin grade) and in-process QC (sheet thickness, tear strength, printability) — define both before the line arrives, not after.

16. Draft your B2B sample/spec sheet — tear resistance, waterproofness, GSM-equivalent weight, printability. See [09_B2B_Client_Acquisition.md](09_B2B_Client_Acquisition.md).

17. **Get 3–5 signed LOIs or pilot POs before the equipment order is placed.** With capex ahead of revenue this is the gate, not a parallel activity ([07_Capital_Plan.md](07_Capital_Plan.md) §9.2.4). Sell against samples sourced or toll-produced elsewhere if you have to — but do not commission a financed line into an empty order book.

## Phase D — The Plant (Weeks 16–32)

18. P leads: lease the unit — Kishangarh/Rajsamand for feedstock proximity and the marble-belt story, with power sanction adequate for extrusion confirmed *before* signing.

19. Order the **extrusion + calendering line** ([07_Capital_Plan.md](07_Capital_Plan.md) §9.2.1, ₹70–95 lakh) — used/refurbished or smaller-capacity is the base case. Never off a single listing: site visit, verified reference customer, independent pre-shipment inspection, and a payment schedule with a real performance-linked retention ([12_Vendor_Hiring_Due_Diligence.md](12_Vendor_Hiring_Due_Diligence.md)).

20. Order the **automatic PLC converting/finishing line** (₹6–12 lakh) — one operator, not a multi-station manual setup. Outsource cover printing locally at first.

21. Hire the line crew: 2–4 trained operators/technicians (ITI/diploma-level, not general labour — [06_Core_Team_Experts.md](06_Core_Team_Experts.md) §8.3), with the process engineer from step 8 on site through commissioning and the first production runs.

22. Commission the line: trial batches → formulation tuning (the 80/18/2 blend is a starting point, not a recipe) → QC against your spec sheet → first saleable production run.

## Phase E — B2B Pipeline (starts Week 6, runs continuously — full playbook in [09_B2B_Client_Acquisition.md](09_B2B_Client_Acquisition.md))

23. Build a target list of 20–30 B2B prospects across packaging converters, FMCG buyers, e-commerce label buyers.

24. Get your first 5 samples out the door with lab-test data attached.

25. Convert samples into signed LOIs and then repeat orders — LOIs gate the capex (step 17); repeat purchase history is what the Stage 2 raise is actually underwritten on ([07_Capital_Plan.md](07_Capital_Plan.md) §9.3.2).

## Phase F — B2C Setup (Weeks 24–36)

26. Launch a simple D2C storefront (Shopify/WooCommerce or an Instagram-led shop) for the stationery line — "made from Kishangarh's marble waste" is your headline story.

27. List on Amazon/Flipkart and relevant marketplaces once you have consistent stock.

28. Use B2C as marketing for the B2B story, not as the primary revenue driver at this stage (per the sequencing logic in [04_Competitive_Landscape.md](04_Competitive_Landscape.md) §5.3).

## Phase G — Run the Plant, Then Raise (Months 8–24+, gated by results, not a calendar)

29. Run the line, sell continuously, and service the Stage 1 debt from operations before anything else. Debt service is the first call on cash — that is what a bootstrapped plant means.

30. Track cost per tonne, cost and margin per SKU, yield/scrap rate and line utilisation from day one ([07_Capital_Plan.md](07_Capital_Plan.md) §9.2.4). This is the dataset the ₹15 crore raise is underwritten on; reconstructing it later costs more than recording it now.

31. Resolve the open items that would otherwise be priced against you: GST/HSN classification advance ruling ([05_Risks_Diligence.md](05_Risks_Diligence.md)), and the Ston2pack post-mortem if step 11 didn't close it.

32. **Only when §9.3.2's conditions are actually met** — 12+ months of production, 3–5 anchor accounts with repeat history, debt serviced on schedule — open the ₹15 crore round. Build the data room out of the operating record, not out of these planning documents.

33. On funding close: 5 TPD line, in-house micronizing, owned site ([07_Capital_Plan.md](07_Capital_Plan.md) §9.3.1). **No new borrowing beyond Stage 1's ₹1.5 crore until at least 50% of that principal is repaid from operations** (§9.4) — the equity round does not lift that gate.

## Quick checklist view

- [ ] Phase A — Pvt Ltd registered with all 3 on it, equity structure settled, trademark filed, financing conversations open
- [ ] Phase B — DPR, process engineer and EHS consultant engaged; Ston2pack diligence closed; three quotes per line item in hand
- [ ] Phase C — Consent to Establish filed, feedstock and HDPE locked, QC defined, **3–5 LOIs signed before any equipment order**
- [ ] Phase D — Unit leased with power sanction, extrusion + converting lines ordered with site visits done, crew hired, line commissioned, first saleable run
- [ ] Phase E — B2B pipeline built, samples out, LOIs converted to repeat orders
- [ ] Phase F — D2C storefront live, marketplace listings up
- [ ] Phase G — Plant running, debt serviced from operations, 12+ months of unit-economics data → ₹15 crore raise opened on that record
