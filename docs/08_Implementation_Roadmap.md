# Implementation Roadmap (Three-Person Team)

*Part of the Limepaper business plan — see [00_README_Index.md](00_README_Index.md). Revised 23 Aug 2026: converting-only first stage gone, Stage 1 is the extrusion plant itself. Revised 31 Aug 2026: Phase F rewritten as a real second revenue channel (own-brand finished goods) against the ten finalized SKUs in [18_Product_SKU_Catalog.md](18_Product_SKU_Catalog.md), not just B2B marketing.*

"F1/F2" = either 18-year-old co-founder — assign by strength. "P" = father, the team's senior member — see [06_Core_Team_Experts.md](06_Core_Team_Experts.md) §8.0 for why his role is formalised. Reflects the strategy in [07_Capital_Plan.md](07_Capital_Plan.md): bootstrapped ₹1.5cr extrusion + converting plant → real operating record → ₹15cr raise to scale to 5 TPD.

**The one structural change to keep in front of you:** capex comes *before* revenue — no cheap trading stage to learn on. Phases A–C exist to de-risk a ₹1.5 crore commitment made before anything is sold.

## Phase A — Foundation (Weeks 1–6)

1. Finalise the one-pager in [01_Core_Idea_Pitch_Summary.md](01_Core_Idea_Pitch_Summary.md) — fill in founder bios (all three).
2. Register the entity — **Pvt Ltd, not LLP** (a ₹15cr equity round is explicit in the plan, [07_Capital_Plan.md](07_Capital_Plan.md) §9.3, and LLPs are a poor vehicle for it). All three formally on it (§8.0), dual-class structure in [15_Equity_Split_Structure.md](15_Equity_Split_Structure.md) settled *before* outside money is discussed.
3. Reserve and trademark-file the brand name.
4. Open a business bank account, set up basic bookkeeping (CA-led).
5. P: open the financing conversation early — get the ₹70L shop formally valued, confirm the bank's LTV, silver-backed loan terms, and CGTMSE-backed term loan appetite with 2–3 banks in parallel; DIC on RIPS 2024/Circular Economy Scheme 2025 slabs ([07_Capital_Plan.md](07_Capital_Plan.md) §9.2.2). Confirm the two existing ₹5L loans (₹70,000 + ₹3.8L outstanding) close cleanly out of this loan's proceeds — lenders want that resolved, not planned. **Sanction lead time is the long pole of this plan** — start week one.
6. F1: supplier-side research — micronized CaCO₃ suppliers in Kishangarh/Rajsamand, HDPE distributors linked to national petrochemical majors. F2: customer-side research (packaging converters, notebook buyers, B2B leads). P: scout a leasable unit with adequate power sanction, build local vendor/landlord relationships.

## Phase B — Core Team & Research (Weeks 4–12, overlaps A)

7. **Engage the DPR consultant properly** ([06_Core_Team_Experts.md](06_Core_Team_Experts.md) role #1) — a ₹1.5cr collateral-backed term loan needs a rigorous, bankable DPR documenting the shop's title/valuation and father's carton-industry experience. No longer deferrable.
8. **Engage the polymer/extrusion process engineer** (role #5) now, as a paid consultant. HDPE–CaCO₃ compounding and extrusion is the core technical risk — temperature profile, die design, additive ratios. Get it wrong and a ₹1cr machine produces brittle or discoloured sheet.
9. Engage the compliance/EHS consultant (role #3) to map the full consent path — PCB Consent to Establish *and* Operate, Udyam/MSME, GST, factory/trade licences. Extrusion triggers all of it; budget the timeline, not just the fee.
10. Competitive-intelligence pass: get samples from Karst, Chinese suppliers, and — if reachable — Ston2pack ([04_Competitive_Landscape.md](04_Competitive_Landscape.md) §5.4) to benchmark real quality/pricing.
11. Try to reach the Ston2pack founder or a past buyer — cheap diligence on why the category's one branded India player went quiet. **Before any equipment order.**
12. Three firm quotes each for: (a) extrusion + calendering line (site visit + reference customer — [12_Vendor_Hiring_Due_Diligence.md](12_Vendor_Hiring_Due_Diligence.md)), (b) converting/finishing equipment, (c) micronized CaCO₃ powder, (d) HDPE resin, (e) tear/waterproof lab certification.

## Phase C — Process, Compliance & Pre-Sales (Weeks 8–20)

13. File every registration from step 9; start the Consent to Establish application — critical path to commissioning.
14. Lock feedstock supply: micronized CaCO₃ agreement with a local unit ([07_Capital_Plan.md](07_Capital_Plan.md) §9.2.3(a)) and an HDPE relationship. **Lab-test every source before committing**, including repeat batches.
15. Set up incoming QC (powder whiteness, particle size, contaminants; resin grade) and in-process QC (sheet thickness, tear strength, printability) before the line arrives.
16. Draft the B2B sample/spec sheet — tear resistance, waterproofness, GSM-equivalent weight, printability. See [09_B2B_Client_Acquisition.md](09_B2B_Client_Acquisition.md).
17. **Get 3–5 signed LOIs or pilot POs before the equipment order.** With capex ahead of revenue this is the gate, not a parallel activity ([07_Capital_Plan.md](07_Capital_Plan.md) §9.2.4). Sell against samples sourced or toll-produced elsewhere if needed — don't commission a financed line into an empty order book.

## Phase D — The Plant (Weeks 16–32)

18. P leads: lease the unit — Kishangarh/Rajsamand for feedstock proximity and the marble-belt story, power sanction adequate for extrusion confirmed *before* signing.
18a. Order the **feedstock refining/micronizing unit** ([07_Capital_Plan.md](07_Capital_Plan.md) §9.2.1, §9.2.3, ₹10–18L) — new capex so purification happens in-house. Same vendor diligence as the extrusion line.
19. Order the **extrusion + calendering line** ([07_Capital_Plan.md](07_Capital_Plan.md) §9.2.1, ₹55–70L) — entry/used tier at ≈80–150 kg/hr is the realistic base case at ₹1.5cr; used/refurbished acceptable with a reference customer and inspection. Never off a single listing: site visit, verified reference, independent pre-shipment inspection, performance-linked retention ([12_Vendor_Hiring_Due_Diligence.md](12_Vendor_Hiring_Due_Diligence.md)).
20. Order the **core converting line** (sheeter/guillotine, die-cutter/creaser, saddle-stitch/binding — [17_Factory_Operations_Plan.md](17_Factory_Operations_Plan.md) §17.2.2, ₹6–10L) — covers notebooks and cartons in one line, one operator. Outsource cover printing locally at first. **The paper bag machine (₹8–15L) is budgeted but ordered separately, only after the glue/rope-attach trial** ([18_Product_SKU_Catalog.md](18_Product_SKU_Catalog.md) §18.9.4) clears. Label die-cutter, wallpaper line, dedicated bakery/QSR equipment all stay later, gated purchases.
21. Hire the line crew: 2–4 trained operators/technicians (ITI/diploma-level, not general labour — [06_Core_Team_Experts.md](06_Core_Team_Experts.md) §8.3), process engineer on site through commissioning and first runs.
22. Commission: trial batches → formulation tuning (80/18/2 is a starting point, not a recipe) → QC against spec sheet → first saleable run.

## Phase E — B2B Pipeline (starts Week 6, runs continuously — full playbook in [09_B2B_Client_Acquisition.md](09_B2B_Client_Acquisition.md))

23. Build a target list of 20–30 B2B prospects across packaging converters, FMCG buyers, e-commerce label buyers.
24. Get first 5 samples out with lab-test data attached.
25. Convert samples into signed LOIs, then repeat orders — LOIs gate the capex (step 17); repeat purchase history underwrites the Stage 2 raise ([07_Capital_Plan.md](07_Capital_Plan.md) §9.3.2).

## Phase F — Own-Brand Finished Goods (Weeks 24–36)

Channel 2 of [03_Unit_Economics.md](03_Unit_Economics.md) §4.7 — finished goods converted in-house, sold under our own name, distinct from Phase E's raw-substrate channel. Runs on the core converting line ([17_Factory_Operations_Plan.md](17_Factory_Operations_Plan.md) §17.2.2), gated by that line's commissioning (Phase D, step 22), not by Phase E.

26. **Convert the plant's own consumption first.** Before external sale, run stationery, sample packaging and outbound mailers on the core converting line — the in-house-use case in §4.7: no sales effort, no markup, quality proven on a captive customer first.
27. Launch a simple D2C storefront (Shopify/WooCommerce or Instagram) for the six SKUs the core line already makes — notebooks, folding/mono-cartons, tags, menus, signage ([18_Product_SKU_Catalog.md](18_Product_SKU_Catalog.md) §18.11) — priced at the standard markup (§18.0.2). "Made from Kishangarh's marble waste" is the headline story.
28. List on Amazon/Flipkart once stock is consistent.
29. Track this channel's revenue and margin per SKU from day one ([07_Capital_Plan.md](07_Capital_Plan.md) §9.2.4) — a real revenue line, not just a B2B marketing funnel. As LOIs and volume justify each §17.2.2 add-on machine (labels, carry bags, mailers, wallpaper), the sellable SKU list grows past the initial six.

## Phase G — Run the Plant, Then Raise (Months 8–24+, gated by results, not a calendar)

30. Run the line, sell continuously, service Stage 1 debt from operations before anything else — the first call on cash in a bootstrapped plant.
31. Track cost per tonne, cost and margin per SKU, yield/scrap rate, line utilisation from day one ([07_Capital_Plan.md](07_Capital_Plan.md) §9.2.4) — the dataset the ₹15cr raise is underwritten on; reconstructing it later costs more than recording it now.
32. Resolve open items that would otherwise be priced against you: GST/HSN advance ruling ([05_Risks_Diligence.md](05_Risks_Diligence.md)), Ston2pack post-mortem if step 11 didn't close it.
33. **Only when §9.3.2's conditions are actually met** — 12+ months of production, 3–5 anchor accounts with repeat history, debt serviced on schedule — open the ₹15cr round. Build the data room from the operating record, not these planning documents.
34. On funding close: 5 TPD line, in-house micronizing at full scale, owned site ([07_Capital_Plan.md](07_Capital_Plan.md) §9.3.1). **No new borrowing beyond Stage 1's ₹1.5cr until ≥50% of that principal is repaid from operations** (§9.4) — the equity round doesn't lift this gate.

## Quick checklist view

- [ ] Phase A — Pvt Ltd registered with all 3 on it, equity structure settled, trademark filed, financing conversations open
- [ ] Phase B — DPR, process engineer, EHS consultant engaged; Ston2pack diligence closed; three quotes per line item in hand
- [ ] Phase C — Consent to Establish filed, feedstock and HDPE locked, QC defined, **3–5 LOIs signed before any equipment order**
- [ ] Phase D — Unit leased with power sanction, extrusion + converting lines ordered with site visits done, crew hired, line commissioned, first saleable run
- [ ] Phase E — B2B pipeline built, samples out, LOIs converted to repeat orders
- [ ] Phase F — In-house consumption running on the core converting line, D2C storefront live for the six core SKUs, marketplace listings up
- [ ] Phase G — Plant running, debt serviced from operations, 12+ months of unit-economics data → ₹15cr raise opened on that record
